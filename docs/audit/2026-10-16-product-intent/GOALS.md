## Current proof classification

No goal in this assessment is newly certified **runtime-proven on current main**. Existing source tests establish intended mechanisms, not fresh results. The historical September dashboard receipt has a different artifact identity. Open PRs remain candidates; their author counts and local checks must not be imported into main's acceptance status.

Coverage is one assurance instrument. A filtered gate percentage is not a product completion percentage, and changing exclusions does not close untested native profiles, required hard cases or unsafe task equivalence. Frozen denominators and independent negative controls remain necessary.

- Confirm local scheduling versus PhenoFabric distributed authority boundaries. Retain automatic heavy-work placement as a requested outcome while avoiding duplicate schedulers.
- Decide FR-013 adoption depth and Airlock parity consumers before turning proposed guards/spec capture into mandatory implementation.
- Resolve source-framing PR913, mature-recovery PR878 and cleanup consumer obligations before further large compression.
- No sunset is approved by this evaluation; unsupported claims should be narrowed without deleting necessary capability.
